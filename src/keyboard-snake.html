Keyboard Snake

Steer with arrows and type the target to arm food.

How to play

  1. Press the arrow keys to steer the snake.
  2. Type the single character shown above the board to arm the food.
  3. Reach the armed, glowing food to grow and score.
  4. Cross an edge to appear on the opposite side of the board.
  5. Press Esc whenever you want to pause.

The beginner pace gives you almost a full second between moves.
