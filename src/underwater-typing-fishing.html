Choose a fish by typing its first letter, then finish the word to reel it in.

How to play

  1. Each fish carries a different word.
  2. Type its first letter to hook it, then complete the word.
  3. A mistake makes the fish pull away but keeps your correct prefix.
  4. Catch the quota at your own pace—fish never escape because time passed.
  5. Press Esc to pause whenever you like.
